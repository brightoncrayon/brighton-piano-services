About Brighton Onyango

Brighton Onyango is a pianist, organist and music educator dedicated to excellence in performance and music learning. His work brings together musical artistry, thoughtful teaching and dependable accompaniment for churches, students and special events.

Since 2022, Brighton has served as an organist at All Saints Cathedral. He also teaches music through the peripatetic programme at Nairobi International School and accompanied performers at the Kenya National Music Festival in 2025 and 2026.

Inspired by the music of Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart, Brighton values musical discipline, expression and the joy of sharing music with others.
